About Us

One man, one reputation, since 2014

Mombo Electronics started as a single Facebook Marketplace listing and grew into a business Bloemfontein trusts for honest, affordable devices.

Our History

Started with one laptop and a Facebook post

Mombo Electronics was founded in 2014 by Idi Mombo, who began selling second-hand and new electronics on platforms like Facebook Marketplace to offer Bloemfontein an affordable way to get quality devices. What started as a one-man operation is still run by Idi today — every device that leaves Mombo Electronics has been checked, priced and sold by the same person, every time.

Over the years, word of mouth has built a loyal customer base who return for laptops, phones and consoles, and who trust the business for its consistent product quality. The range has grown to cover everything from budget student laptops to the latest consoles — always at prices that make sense.
